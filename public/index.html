      Optional access to your private YouTube playlists and videos. Stillroom uses your existing signed-in session; it never asks for your Google password. Enable it separately for each import.

      Sign into YouTube in the browser profile you used most recently, or use a cookie file to choose an account precisely. Close that browser before importing if access fails. macOS may ask to unlock browser cookies in Keychain; Safari may need Full Disk Access.
